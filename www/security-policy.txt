# Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

Designers Guild values the work of ethical security researchers. However, we do not operate a monetary bug bounty programme and will not engage with "beg-bounty" tactics.

### Reward Policy
* We do not offer financial compensation, cash rewards, or bounties for vulnerability reports.
* We do not provide "certificates of appreciation" or LinkedIn recommendations for standard reports.
* Well-documented, valid, and high-severity reports may receive a formal email expression of gratitude from our team.

### Excluded Submission Types (Beg-Bounty Filters)
We will immediately close and ignore reports generated by automated scanners without proof of a manual exploit. We do not accept or respond to the following low-impact findings:
1. Missing HTTP security headers (e.g., Strict-Transport-Security, CSP, X-Frame-Options) without a working exploit.
2. Missing SPF, DKIM, or DMARC email records.
3. Cookie flags (e.g., Secure, HttpOnly) missing on non-sensitive cookies.
4. SSL/TLS best practices or cipher suite recommendations.
5. Server error pages that expose version numbers but do not leak sensitive user data.

By submitting a report to security@designersguild.com, you agree to these terms.
